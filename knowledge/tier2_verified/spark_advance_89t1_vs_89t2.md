# `1989` `2.5L Turbo I` vs. `2.2L Turbo II` Total (`MAP+RPM`) Spark Advance Matrix

Direct comparison of factory combined `MAP + RPM` timing tables across `1000–8000 RPM` and `+14 psi` boost to `-14 psi` vacuum (`deg BTDC`). All numbers match `89T1vs89T2sparkadvance.pdf` p.1.

## 1. Key Calibration Contrasts (`T1` Non-Intercooled Long-Stroke vs. `T2` Intercooled)

- **Low/Mid-Range Transition (`1500–4000 RPM`, `6` to `0 psi`)**: The `2.5L Turbo I` calibration runs **`6.0°–10.5°` LESS advance** than `2.2L Turbo II` across `+6 psi` down through `0 psi` at `1500–2500 RPM` (`-10.5°` max delta at `2000 RPM, +6 psi`), reflecting `2.5L` higher compression/non-intercooled knock sensitivity and earlier torque peak.
- **High-RPM Taper (`5500–8000 RPM`)**: Above `5500 RPM`, `2.2L Turbo II` levels off at **`12.0°` (`8–14 psi`)** to **`32.0°` (`-6 to -14 psi`)**, whereas `2.5L Turbo I` climbs to **`24.0°–25.5°` (`8–14 psi`)** and **`45.0°` (`-8 to -14 psi`)**, yielding **`+12.0°` to `+13.5°` MORE advance** above `6000 RPM`.
